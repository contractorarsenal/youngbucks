Seasonal Priority

Sprinkler Blowouts & Winterization

Wenatchee Valley winters bring sustained freezing temperatures, and irrigation systems that hold water in their lines are at risk when that happens. A seasonal blowout uses compressed air to clear water from pipes, valves and heads before hard freezes set in.

Scheduling before the weather turns matters more than the exact calendar date. We work through the valley zone by zone as fall progresses so property owners aren't caught out by an early cold snap.

Seasonal Priority

Every job includes full cleanup — brush chipped on site, logs cut to rounds or hauled away, the property left better than we found it.
