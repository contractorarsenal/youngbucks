What Most Homeowners Get Wrong About Retaining Walls

Central Washington slopes, freeze-thaw cycles and clay-heavy soil mean a retaining wall has to do more than look good. Most wall failures start behind the wall, not in front of it.

The wall is only as good as what's behind it.
Learn About Retaining Walls

Proper Base

Compacted aggregate base carries the load. Skip it and the wall moves.

Drainage

Drainage rock and perforated pipe move water away from the wall, not through it.

Structural Support

Wall height, batter and reinforcement engineered for the actual slope, not guessed at.
