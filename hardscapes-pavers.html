A patio is only as good as what's underneath it.

We've torn out patio work that started sinking in the first year — improperly compacted base, no drainage, shortcuts throughout. We get the base prep and drainage right before the first stone goes down, so the result holds up and looks sharp for decades.

Custom Installs

Paver Patios

Custom paver patio installations using concrete pavers, natural stone, or flagstone. Every project includes proper excavation, base compaction, drainage, and precision laying so the surface stays level and locked for decades.

Built for Freeze-Thaw

Walkways & Driveways

Paver walkways and driveways that look as good as they perform. We design for the load, drainage, and look of your property — built to handle Wenatchee's freeze-thaw cycles without heaving.

Solid & Safe
