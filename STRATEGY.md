## 5. CTA Recommendations

### Primary CTAs (every page):
- **"Get Free Estimate"**: links to contact.html
- **"Call (509) 555-0100"**: `tel:` link

### Placement Strategy:
- **Hero section:** Both CTAs side by side (primary + phone)
- **Emergency banner:** Phone call CTA (prominent red section)
- **After each major service section:** One inline CTA
- **Sidebar widget on every inner page:** Both CTAs
- **End of every page:** Full CTA band (both CTAs)
- **Navigation:** Free Estimate button (gold) + phone number always visible

### CTA Hierarchy:
1. **Get Free Estimate** (gold button): highest intent action
2. **Call Now** (outline/phone button): immediate/emergency intent
3. **Learn More** (text link): lower-funnel, sends to service page

## 10. Brand Voice Guidelines

**Tone:** Direct. Confident. Blue-collar professional. Local authority.

**Use:**
- Specific, concrete language ("four hours" not "quickly")
- Regional references (Wenatchee Heights, valley, Eastern Washington winters)
- Problem/solution framing ("Walls fail because of water. Here is how we solve it.")
- First person plural ("We," "Our crew," "We've been doing this for 12 years")

**Avoid:**
- "We're passionate about..."
- "We take pride in..."
- "Best-in-class" or similar empty claims
- Corporate-speak or industry jargon without explanation
- Exclamation points used for enthusiasm (only for emergency/urgent contexts)
- Vague superlatives without substantiation

## 14. Form Handler Setup

- **Custom backend:** Handle POST submission server-side
