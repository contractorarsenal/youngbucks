Routine care

A lawn that is handled, visit after visit.

Regular lawn care is the most common thing people ask us for. Some do not have the time. Some cannot do the work anymore. Some are away from the property. The job is the same: show up on schedule and leave the lawn cut and clean.

Every Visit

Mowing, Edging & Trimming

The lawn is mowed, the edges along beds and walks are cut, and the spots a mower cannot reach are trimmed. Your written estimate lists what each visit includes.

Seasonal

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups, and first-visit cleanups for yards that have gotten ahead of their owners. Tell us the condition of the yard and we will scope the cleanup before regular service starts.

Good to know

Water is half of lawn care

In a dry summer, a lawn is only as good as its sprinkler coverage. If zones are missing spots or heads are broken, mowing will not fix it. We handle irrigation repair and fall sprinkler blowouts too, so one company can look at both.
