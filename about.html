Where It Started

Built by a father and son. Grown in the Wenatchee Valley.

Young Bucks Landscaping started in 2013 as a father-and-son mowing crew in the Wenatchee Valley. It began simply: a lawn mowing route, a handful of regular customers, and a commitment to show up and do the job right.

In the second year, the business expanded into professional tree care, with lineman-level safety built into how the crew approaches every tree. From there, Young Bucks kept growing into landscaping, irrigation, retaining walls, hardscapes, and the other outdoor services it offers today. The equipment and the crew have grown a lot since 2013. What hasn't changed is that it's still a local, family-owned company doing the work in the valley it started in.
