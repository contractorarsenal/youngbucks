Water Management

Wenatchee gets hot and dry. Your lawn doesn't have to.

A properly designed irrigation system keeps your lawn green without overwatering, runoff, or wasted money on your water bill. Need seasonal winterization instead of a new install or repair? Visit our sprinkler winterization page.

New Installs

Irrigation System Installation

Complete irrigation system design and installation for residential and commercial properties. Zone layout, head placement, valve box installation, controller setup, and system testing, all included. We design every system zone by zone based on your soil, sun exposure, and plant types.

Repairs

Irrigation Repair & Troubleshooting

Broken heads, leaking valves, stuck zones, controller issues. We diagnose the problem, fix it properly, and make sure the system performs before we leave.

Smart Tech

Smart Controller Upgrades

Upgrade your system to a smart controller that adjusts watering schedules based on weather data, soil moisture, and evapotranspiration rates. Water less, save money, keep things green.

Seasonal Priority

Startup & Winterization (Blowouts)

Spring system startup includes checking every zone, adjusting heads, and programming the controller. Fall blowouts push compressed air through every zone to clear water before freeze. Full details, FAQ and scheduling live on our dedicated winterization page.

Efficiency & Commercial

System Audits & Commercial Irrigation

Full irrigation system audits identify inefficiencies, misfiring heads, overspray, and scheduling issues — we give you a report and fix what we find. We also design and install large-scale systems for commercial properties, HOAs, and new construction.
