Water management

Wenatchee gets hot and dry. Your lawn doesn't have to.

A properly designed irrigation system keeps your lawn green without overwatering, runoff, or wasted money on your water bill. We design every system zone by zone, head by head, based on your specific soil, sun exposure, and plant types.

And when fall arrives, we blow out your system properly so no water is left in the lines to freeze and crack. Get it wrong and you're replacing heads and valves every spring. Get it right and your system runs for a decade without issues.

New Installs

Irrigation System Installation

Complete irrigation system design and installation for residential and commercial properties. Zone layout, head placement, valve box installation, controller setup, and system testing. All included.

Repairs

Irrigation Repair & Troubleshooting

Broken heads, leaking valves, stuck zones, controller issues. We diagnose the problem, fix it properly, and make sure the system performs before we leave. No guesswork, no callback charges for the same issue.

Smart Tech

Smart Controller Upgrades

Upgrade your system to a smart controller that adjusts watering schedules based on weather data, soil moisture, and evapotranspiration rates. Water less, save money, keep things green.

Seasonal

Startup & Winterization (Blowouts)

Spring system startup includes checking every zone, adjusting heads, and programming the controller. Fall blowouts push compressed air through every zone to clear all water before freeze. Done right, every time.

Efficiency

System Audits & Water Savings

Full irrigation system audit to identify inefficiencies, misfiring heads, overspray, and scheduling issues. We give you a report and fix what we find. A wasteful system becomes an efficient one.

Commercial Irrigation

Large-scale irrigation for commercial properties, HOAs, and new construction. We design and install systems that handle the scale and uptime requirements of commercial and multi-property accounts.
