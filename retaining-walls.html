Why Walls Fail

The wall isn't the problem. The drainage is.

We've rebuilt retaining walls that didn't make it two winters — poor drainage behind them, wrong base, wrong block for the load. Wenatchee's freeze-thaw cycle is brutal on walls without proper drainage, so we put drainage aggregate and perforated pipe behind every wall we build. No exceptions.

Every Wall, Properly Engineered

What Separates a Wall That Holds

01

Drainage Aggregate

Behind every wall we build. It's what separates a wall that holds from one that fails in three winters. We never skip this step.

02

Perforated Pipe

Installed at the footing to move water away from the wall base and prevent saturation of the retained soil.

03

Compacted Base

We excavate to undisturbed soil, compact the base, and size the footing for the height and load.
