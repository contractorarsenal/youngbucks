Common questions

Tree removal FAQ

How much does tree removal cost in Wenatchee?+
Tree removal pricing depends on the size, location, and complexity of the job. A small tree in an open yard is very different from a large dead pine hanging over a roof. Most residential removals run from $400 to $2,500+. We give free on-site estimates so you know the exact number before we touch anything.
Do you do emergency same-day tree removal?+
We respond quickly to active hazards, including trees on roofs, blocked driveways, and limbs hanging on power lines. Call us directly at (509) 470-5684 and we'll assess the situation. You'll always know the cost before we start.
Do you haul away the debris and wood?+
Yes. Full cleanup is included. Brush gets chipped on site, logs get cut to rounds or hauled away, your preference. The site looks better when we leave than when we arrived. No extra charge for cleanup.
Are you licensed and insured for tree work in Washington State?+
Yes. We carry full general liability insurance on every job. We're happy to provide a certificate of insurance before any work begins. Don't hire a tree company without verifying this. It protects your property if anything goes wrong.
Can you remove trees close to my house or power lines?+
Yes. Technical tree work near structures is our specialty. It requires proper rigging, sectional takedowns, and experienced judgment. We've removed trees from situations that other companies turned down. We'll assess it and tell you exactly how we'd approach it.
