About Ephrata

Verified local context

  • Ephrata is the seat of Grant County, a role it has held since the county was created in 1909.
  • Recent census counts put Ephrata at about 8,500 residents.
  • The Quincy-Columbia Basin Irrigation District's service area includes Ephrata.
  • The Columbia Basin region receives about 6 to 10 inches of rain a year, mostly in winter and early spring. Without irrigation, most crops would not grow in this area.

Sources: Wikipedia: Ephrata, Washington; Grant County history; Citypopulation.de; National Park Service, Columbia Basin history.
